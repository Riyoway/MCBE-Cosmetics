# Third-party material

The cape archive used as the current migration source is not original artwork by Riyoway.

Its original Bedrock manifests identify **Xatalyst** as the pack creator and state that cape material was provided by **Badlion**. The archive itself did not include a license file.

Riyoway's work in this repository covers maintenance, compatibility updates, organization and tooling unless a specific asset says otherwise.

Do not mark the imported cape textures as MIT, CC0, or another open-source/content license without confirming the rights for those assets first.

When an original author or source is known, preserve that attribution in the pack and documentation.
